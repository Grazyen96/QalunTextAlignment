# Qalun — a guide for everyone

Qalun turns a recording of a Quran recitation or a lecture into a video. The
Quran text appears on screen, and each word lights up at the moment it is
spoken.

You don't need to know anything about computers or programming to use it. This
guide covers everything you need.

---

## What Qalun does for you

- **Listens to your recording** and works out which ayat are being recited.
- **Shows the ayat on screen** in the Qaloon script, with each word lighting up
  as it is heard.
- **Lets you check and fix** anything it got wrong before the video is made.
- **Makes the video**, ready to upload to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and other
  platforms.
- **Writes subtitle files** too, if you want them.
- **Adds an intro and an outro** for a lecture, with your name or channel and,
  if you like, a QR code that leads viewers to your website.

It works with two kinds of recording:

| Kind | What it is | What Qalun shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Recitation** | Someone reciting the Quran | The ayat being recited, word by word |
| **Lecture** | A talk or lesson that quotes the Quran | The ayat of the surah being studied, each one as it is quoted |

## Getting started

### 1. Download

Go to the
[Releases page](https://github.com/Grazyen96/QalunTextAlignment/releases) and
download the file for your computer (Windows, Mac or Linux).

### 2. Unzip and open

Unzip the file you downloaded and double-click **Qalun**. That's it: there is
nothing else to install.

### 3. The first time only

The first time you make a video, Qalun downloads its listening tool (about
110 MB). This happens **only once**, so you need an internet connection that
first time. After that, Qalun works fully offline.

### What your computer needs

- **Memory:** at least 4 GB. On a computer with only 4 GB, close other large
  programs (browsers with many tabs, for example) before starting a long
  lecture.
- **Space:** about 300 MB free.
- **Internet:** only once, for the first download.

## Making your first video

### Step 1: Start a new job

When Qalun opens, you'll see a list of your videos on the left (empty the
first time) and a form on the right.

1. **Drop your audio file** onto the form, or click to choose it.
2. **Choose the kind:** *Recitation* or *Lecture*.
3. **Pick the surah and ayat** if you know them. If you don't, leave it on
   *Detect automatically* and Qalun will work it out.
4. **Choose a video size**:
   - *Portrait* (upright) for phones: Instagram, TikTok, Shorts.
   - *Landscape 1920×1080* for YouTube.
   - *Landscape 1280×720* for a smaller file.
   - *Quick draft* to see a fast first version.
5. Press **Start**.

You'll see progress and an estimate of how long is left. Press **Cancel** at any
time; nothing is left behind.

### Step 2: Check the result

When Qalun has finished listening, the **review window** opens by itself. Here
you watch a preview and fix anything before the video is made.

- Press **Space** to play and pause.
- Watch the words light up. If a word lights up too early or too late, select
  it and nudge it with the **,** and **.** keys.
- Made a mistake? Press **Ctrl+Z** to undo.
- Press **Ctrl+S** to save your corrections.
- Press **?** to see every shortcut.

The word timeline runs **right to left**, like the Arabic text, so the start of
the recording is on the right.

If something needs your attention (for example, a stretch of the recording
where no word was found), a notice appears at the top of the window.

### Step 3: Choose the look

In the **Look** panel beside the preview, choose one of six colour styles:

- Night gold (the default)
- Emerald
- Sand
- Ink on paper (light background, dark text)
- Ocean
- Rose

You can also change the background: a plain colour, a gradient, a pattern, or
your own picture. The preview changes right away, and the finished video looks
exactly like the preview.

If you like a look, click **Use for new videos** and every new video will start
with it.

### Step 4: Make the video

Press **Render**. When it's done, a notice appears with **Play** and **Show in
folder** buttons so you can watch the video or find the file.

## Lectures: a few extra things

### Which surah appears on screen

A lecture usually studies one surah but may quote ayat from others. Qalun shows
**only the surah being studied** on the on-screen card. Ayat quoted from other
surahs appear in the subtitles instead.

Qalun picks the surah it hears the most. To change it, use **Surah on the
cards** in the review window.

### Showing an ayah the teacher explains but doesn't recite

Sometimes a teacher talks about an ayah without reading it aloud. To show it
anyway, move to that moment in the recording and click **Show ayah… from
here**. Choose the ayah and it stays on screen until the next one. Cards you
place yourself are marked with ✎.

### Intro, recitation and outro (an "episode")

Click **Episode…** in the review window to put together a complete episode:

1. **Intro**: your own clip, or one Qalun makes for you showing your name and
   the surah and ayat.
2. **Recitation**: one of your recitation videos.
3. **The lecture** itself.
4. **Outro**: your own clip, or one Qalun makes with a closing phrase and,
   if you want, a QR code to your website.

When you render the lecture, Qalun also makes the joined-up episode as a
separate video. Your lecture video is always kept on its own as well.

### The QR code and your website link

If you add a website link to the outro, Qalun turns it into a QR code. Tell
Qalun where you're publishing (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Telegram
or WhatsApp).

The QR code carries a small hidden label so that your website statistics can
show **how many people came from each video and each platform**. The **Copy**
button gives you a matching link for the video description, labelled
separately, so you can see whether people came from the QR code or from the
description.

The address written under the QR code is kept short and plain so viewers can
read and type it.

## Settings

Click the **gear** at the top of the window.

- **General:** language (English or Arabic), light or dark appearance.
- **Transcription:** how Qalun listens to your recording. Leave it on *this
  computer* unless you've been told otherwise.
- **Files:** where your finished videos are saved.
- **Videos:** the look, intro and outro new videos start with.
- **About:** the version number and the error log.

The **sun/moon** button at the top switches between light and dark.

## Finding your videos

Every video you've made appears in the list on the left of the main window.
Each one shows a small picture, its name and where it stands:

- **Needs review**: Qalun has finished listening and is waiting for you to check.
- **Reviewed**: you've checked it but haven't made the video yet.
- **Rendered**: the video is ready.
- **Changed since the last render**: you edited it after making the video;
  render again to update it.

Double-click a job to open it again. Right-click for **Show in folder** to find
the files.

**Remove from list** only hides a job from the list; your files are not deleted.

## When something goes wrong

**The video has the wrong ayat.**
Start the job again and pick the surah and ayat yourself instead of *Detect
automatically*.

**The computer slows down or Qalun closes during a long lecture.**
Close other programs and try again. Long lectures need more memory.

**The first job is stuck on "downloading".**
Check your internet connection. The download only happens once.

**Something else.**
Go to **Settings → About → Open log folder** and send the file `qalun.log`
to whoever helps you with Qalun. It records what went wrong.
